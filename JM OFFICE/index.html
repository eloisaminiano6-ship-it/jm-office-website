What is JM Office and School Supplies Trading?

JM Office School Supplies Trading originated in 2019 from Miniano Printing Services. It was a small business that initially offered a few basic services like souvenir printing, silk screen printing, and mural painting for their clients. As they grew over the years, they expanded further into what they offer today. They changed their name to JM Office School Supplies Trading in 2023 to highlight their expanded scope of services. At present, the company offers tarpaulin printing, plaques, trophies, medals, signages, T-shirt printing, sublimation, and corporate souvenirs. The company sells different supplies, which cater to both government organizations and private companies. Being a Platinum member of PhilGEPS, the company participates in government procurement and bidding. Apart from being involved in many government projects, the company has been known to create booths and event installation works for various festivities such as Christmas Village and local fiestas.

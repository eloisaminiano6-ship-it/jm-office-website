What is JM Office and School Supplies Trading?

JM Office School Supplies Trading began in 2019 as Miniano Printing Services, offering souvenir printing, silk screen printing, and mural painting. As the business grew, it expanded its services and officially became JM Office School Supplies Trading in 2023.
Today, the company provides tarpaulin printing, plaques, trophies, medals, signages, T-shirt printing, sublimation, corporate souvenirs, and office supplies for both government and private clients. As a Platinum PhilGEPS member, the company proudly serves government projects and creates booths and event installations for local festivals and community celebrations.

Services

🎨 DTF Printing

We offer high-quality DTF (Direct-to-Film) printing for T-shirts, hoodies, tote bags, and more. Enjoy vibrant colors, durable prints, and sharp details that last through everyday wear and washing.

🖌️ Murals

Bring your walls to life with custom mural paintings. We create creative and meaningful designs for schools, resorts, businesses, and public spaces that leave a long lasting impression.

📝 Signages

We design and produce durable outdoor signages that help your business stand out. We make them clear, professional, and built to last.

☕ Customized Mugs

Looking for a unique gift or souvenir? Our customized mugs are perfect for birthdays, company giveaways, school events, and special occasions.

🥇 Medals

Celebrate every achievement with custom-made medals for sports competitions, academic events, and recognition programs. Designed to make every award memorable.

🖨️ Tarpaulin Printing

We print high-quality tarpaulins for birthdays, graduations, business promotions, school activities, and other special events.

🏆 Customized Trophies

Celebrate every success with our customized trophies, perfect for sports events, academic competitions, company recognition, and special awards.

👔 T-Shirt Printing

We create custom T-shirts for schools, organizations, businesses, and special events. Whether you need uniforms or personalized designs, we make sure every print is made with quality.
